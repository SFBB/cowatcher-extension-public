# YouTube Platform Guide

CoWatcher brings AI bilingual subtitles and vocabulary study to YouTube.

---

## Core Features

- **Creator Subtitles & Automatic Speech Recognition (ASR)**:
  - Intelligently intercepts creator-provided subtitle tracks.
  - Automatically merges fragmented YouTube auto-generated captions (ASR) into coherent syntactic sentences before dispatching them to LLMs for translation.
- **SPA Route & Playlist Navigation**: Listens to in-page video switches, recommendations, and playlist progressions, reloading metadata and subtitles automatically.
- **Ad Compatibility**: Mutes and pauses subtitle processing during YouTube video ads.

---

## Tips

- Ensure closed captions (CC) are enabled in YouTube's native player.
- Toggle bilingual subtitles anytime using **`Alt + T`**.
