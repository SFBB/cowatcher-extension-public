# Netflix Platform Guide

CoWatcher is deeply integrated with Netflix's proprietary Kadmium player architecture.

---

## Core Features

- **Simultaneous Dual Subtitles**: Displays original audio transcript alongside AI-translated subtitles, or vice versa.
- **Segment Clock Alignment**: Automatically aligns with Netflix internal atomic video segment offsets, ensuring instant synchronization upon seeking.
- **Native Subtitle Suppression**: Automatically conceals single-track Netflix native subtitles when AI bilingual mode is active to prevent visual clutter.

---

## Tips & Shortcuts

1. **Global Hotkey**: Press **`Alt + T`** anywhere on Netflix to toggle bilingual subtitles.
2. **Swap Order**: Toggle **Swap Subtitle Order** in the extension popup to place original text on the bottom and translated text on top.
3. **Interactive Vocabulary**: Hover over unfamiliar words in the original track to inspect pronunciation, definitions, and save to your study deck.
